OUR SERVICES

Ferum Technical is the solution partner of its customers in the fields of engineering design, welding technology, procurement services, material inspection and technical training.

Welding Technology

Ferum Technical acts as a welding consultant for a variety of manufacturing industries. Our experienced team supports clients from project planning until completion, ensuring welding quality, compliance and operational efficiency.

We provide consultation, inspection and technical support across multiple welding disciplines and international standards.

  • Weld Design & Welding Metallurgy
  • Codes & Standards Interpretation
  • Quality Assurance & Quality Control
  • Qualification of WPS & WQT
  • Reverse Engineering Services
  • Independent Design Review
  • Supply of Technical Manpower
  • Supply of Material & Welding Tools

Engineering Inspection

Ferum Technical provides complete Engineering Inspection services for fabricated equipment, piping systems, pressure vessels, structural steel and manufacturing components.

We assist clients from procurement, fabrication, inspection, testing and final acceptance to ensure compliance with project specifications and international standards.

  • Incoming Material Inspection
  • Fabrication Inspection
  • Pressure Vessel Inspection
  • Pipe & Structural Inspection
  • Factory Acceptance Test (FAT)
  • Site Acceptance Test (SAT)
  • Quality Documentation Review

Material Testing

Ferum Technical provides comprehensive material testing services to verify the quality, strength and performance of metallic and non-metallic materials. We support manufacturers, contractors and industrial clients by ensuring compliance with international standards and project specifications.

Our testing services help clients improve product reliability, maintain quality assurance and ensure materials are suitable for their intended applications.

  • Mechanical Testing
  • Tensile & Hardness Testing
  • Corrosion Resistance Testing
  • Metallurgical Analysis
  • Conventional Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)
  • Advanced Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)
  • Failure Analysis

Technical Training

We provide professional technical training programmes designed to improve employee competency, workplace safety and engineering knowledge. Our practical approach combines classroom learning with hands-on industrial experience.

Training programmes can be customized according to organizational requirements and industry standards.

  • Quality Assurance & Quality Control
  • Engineering Codes & Standards (ASME, AWS, API, ISO)
  • Health, Safety & Environment (HSE)
  • Welding Inspection
  • Welding Technology
  • Metallurgy Fundamentals
  • Technical Skills Development
