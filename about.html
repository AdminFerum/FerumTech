ABOUT FERUM TECHNICAL

We are more than just a brand, we are a collective of passionate individuals driven by a common purpose. Our dedication to innovation and continuous improvement is the cornerstone of our success.

OUR STORY

Ferum Technical was established in Kuala Lumpur in 2021. With acquired deep knowledge and expertise, it has a growing and evolving vision in consultation and engineering.

Ferum Technical is the solution partner of its customers in the fields of engineering design, welding technology, procurement services, material inspection and technical training.
